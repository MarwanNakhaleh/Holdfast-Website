Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: we collect almost nothing, on purpose. The Holdfast Brick is designed so that your data physically cannot reach us. This page explains the small amount of information that does exist when you use this website or reserve a brick.

The brick itself

Once a brick is in your home, all of your data, photos, passwords, and browsing stay on your own hardware and your own internet connection. The brick has no cloud service, no accounts, and no telemetry. It does not phone home to us. Software updates are downloaded by your brick directly from the original software makers (such as Debian and GitHub), not from us, so we never see your IP address or usage.

What this website collects

  • Reservations: if you place a refundable reservation, our payment processor (Stripe) handles your card details and shares your email address and name with us so we can contact you about your reservation. We never see your full card number.
  • Emails you send us: if you email support, we keep your message and address so we can reply.
  • Aggregate ad statistics: if you arrived from an ad, the ad platform (such as Meta) may report anonymous conversion counts to us. We receive no personal data from this.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.
  • We do not run analytics, tracking pixels that identify you, or cookies beyond what the payment processor requires.
  • We do not email you marketing unless you specifically ask us to.

Deleting your information

Email hello@holdfastbrick.com and we will delete your reservation record and email history, and confirm the deletion. Your brick itself never contained our data in the first place.

Questions

Email hello@holdfastbrick.com. A human reads it.
