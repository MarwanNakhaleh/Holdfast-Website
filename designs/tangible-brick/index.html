How it works

Three steps, in order

This is a real sequence — each step builds on the one before it. Most people are done inside an afternoon.

  1. 1

    Plug it in

    Connect the brick to any LAN port on your router with an ethernet cable and plug in the power. Give it a minute to boot. Wired on purpose — the brick serves your whole network, so it never depends on WiFi.

  2. 2

    Pair the app

    Open the iOS companion app on the same WiFi. It finds the brick on your network by itself — you just enter the 6-digit pairing code printed at the end of the install.

  3. 3

    Whole home protected

    Turn on whole-home protection in the app. A guided flow checks your network and hands address duty to the brick — after that every device is covered automatically, including guests.

Straight answers

What it blocks — and what it can't

The brick runs AdGuard Home with encrypted DNS lookups (DNS-over-TLS via Unbound). That covers a lot. It doesn't cover everything, and we'd rather tell you now.

What it kills

  • ✓Web ads, on every browser in the house
  • ✓In-app banner ads on phones and tablets
  • ✓Trackers and telemetry phoning home
  • ✓Smart-TV home-screen ads

What it can't promise

In-stream video ads on streaming services — Disney+, Hulu, YouTube — are stitched into the video on the server side. DNS blocking can't reliably remove them — it's hit-or-miss depending on the service, device, and blocklist.

If a product promises otherwise, ask how. We'd rather be the brick that's honest about it.
