Design directions

Five takes on the Holdfast landing page. Each is a complete, self-contained page; the one at the site root is the current pick — swap it by copying any variant's index.html over the root one.

  1. The front panel Network-hardware idiom: status LEDs, etched labels, PCB navy and copper.
  2. The tangible brick Chunky, toy-like physicality — a CSS-built brick you can almost hold.
  3. The query log A live DNS ledger as the hero: trackers struck through in real time, on paper-light ground.
  4. The household schematic An installer's annotated wiring diagram of your home, drawn in SVG.
  5. The quiet home Dusk tones and one orchestrated moment: the noise fades, the home goes quiet.
