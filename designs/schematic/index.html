Sheet 1 of 1 — whole-home ad blocking

One box on the router.
Every device covered.

Holdfast is a small device — a Raspberry Pi — that plugs into your home router and blocks ads and trackers for every device on your network: phones, TVs, laptops, guests. No app installs per device, no subscriptions, no cloud account. Your data never leaves your home. The whole thing fits on one drawing.

Section 2 — install sequence

Three steps. One evening. Done.

The order matters, which is why it's numbered. Everything here is the real procedure — the full walkthrough with screenshots lives in the setup guide.

  1. Flash the SD card

    Install Raspberry Pi Imager on your computer and choose Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit). In the Imager's settings (the gear icon), enable SSH and set a username and password. Write the card, then put it in the Pi.

  2. Plug the brick into your router

    Connect the ethernet cable from the Pi to any LAN port on your router, then plug in power. Give it a minute to boot. Wired is required — the brick will serve your whole network and must not depend on WiFi.

  3. Run one script, then pair the app

    Find the Pi's address in your router's device list (or try ssh <username>@raspberrypi.local), then:

    ssh <username>@<pi-address>
git clone https://github.com/MarwanNakhaleh/PrivacyBrickAPI.git
cd PrivacyBrickAPI
sudo bash deploy/provision.sh

    The script installs and wires everything — AdGuard Home, Unbound with encrypted DNS, and the Holdfast API — and it's safe to re-run any time. Finish AdGuard's one-time wizard at http://<pi-address>:3000 (web port 3000, DNS port 53), then run the script once more. After that, the iOS app finds the brick on your WiFi by itself — enter the 6-digit pairing code printed at the end of the install, and flip on whole-home protection from the app.

Section 3 — what dies at the brick, and what doesn't

Inspected and passed — mostly

Stopped at the brick

  • Web ads, on every browser in the house
  • In-app banner ads
  • Trackers and telemetry phoning home
  • Smart-TV home-screen ads
  • All of the above for guests, automatically

Streaming video ads: hit or miss

In-stream video ads on streaming services — Disney+, Hulu, YouTube — are stitched into the video server-side and can't be reliably blocked by DNS. Nothing that works the way Holdfast works can promise to remove them — sometimes a blocklist catches them, sometimes it doesn't. Anything that claims a guaranteed fix deserves a hard look.

Everything in the left column, though, is gone.
