What a reservation is

A $15 reservation holds your place in line for the first production run of the Holdfast Brick. The full $15 comes off your final purchase price. This is a reservation, not a prepayment for a promised delivery date.

Your money

  • Fully refundable at any time, for any reason, no questions asked. Email hello@holdfastbrick.com and it is returned to your original payment method within 5 business days.
  • We will never charge the remaining balance without your explicit, separate confirmation. The reservation is the only payment this page takes.
  • If we ever decide not to produce the brick, every reservation is refunded automatically and you will be told directly.

What happens after you reserve

Reservations are filled in the order they were placed as production units become available. We will contact you by email before any payment is due, and you can decline at that point and keep or refund your reservation.

What the brick is

A small fanless computer pre-loaded with open-source software (including AdGuard Home, Unbound, and Immich) that blocks ads and trackers on your home network and hosts your photos and passwords locally. It works with your existing router and requires no subscription.

Honest limitations

  • The brick blocks ads delivered over the network. It cannot remove ads that streaming services play inside their own video streams, Spotify audio ads, or sponsored content.
  • Photos and passwords stored on the brick live on your hardware. You are responsible for backups; a backup feature is planned.
  • Ad blocking is an ongoing effort: blocklists are updated continuously, but no DNS device can guarantee every ad is blocked forever.

Warranty

Units sold at launch carry a 1-year hardware warranty: if the hardware fails within a year of arrival, we replace it. This reservation itself is refundable and is not a warranty claim.
