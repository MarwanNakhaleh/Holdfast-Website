Common questions

Do I need to be technical? No. You plug the brick into your router with one cable, install a trust profile on your phone, and pair from the app. The app runs everything else.

Where does my data go? Nowhere. There is no cloud, no account, no telemetry. Photos and passwords live on the brick inside your home. Even updates come straight from the software makers, not from us.

What do I need? Any router with a spare network port. The brick handles the rest.

What's the hardware? A fanless mini-computer (Intel N150, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) that idles at a few watts, about the size of a paperback book.
